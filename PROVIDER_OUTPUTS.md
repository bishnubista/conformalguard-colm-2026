# Provider Score Outputs

The files `results/llm_scores_claude_v2.json` and
`results/llm_scores_gpt_v2.json` contain pseudonymous identifiers, numeric
classifier scores, latency measurements, numeric response strings, and one
sanitized API error. They contain no email bodies or generated prose.

These files are distributed to support verification of the published results.
They are not covered by the MIT license in `LICENSE`, and this repository does
not grant additional reuse rights for them. Any reuse must comply with the
applicable provider terms and must be limited to rights the user already has.
The authors do not relicense model-provider intellectual property.

The third-party email corpora are not included. Their separate terms and
privacy considerations are described in `THIRD_PARTY_DATA.md`.
